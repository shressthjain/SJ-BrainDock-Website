Your data, your control

BrainDock is built privacy-first. All processing happens locally on your device. We never see, store, or share your personal data.

How BrainDock Handles Data

100% Local Processing

All AI models run directly on your device. Your screen content, keystrokes, and app usage data never leave your computer.

End-to-End Encryption

Any data that syncs between your devices is encrypted end-to-end. Not even BrainDock can access your encrypted content.

Delete Anytime

Export or permanently delete all your BrainDock data at any time from the app settings. No questions, no delays.

No Screen Recording

BrainDock analyzes focus patterns without recording, storing, or transmitting your screen content. Ever.

Granular Permissions

Choose exactly which apps BrainDock can monitor. Exclude sensitive applications like banking, health, or personal apps.

Enterprise Controls

Admins can set organization-wide data policies, retention periods, and compliance configurations for all team members.

Data Architecture

Built for privacy from day one

BrainDock's architecture was designed so that your personal data never reaches our servers.

  • ✓ AI models run on-device using Apple Silicon / GPU acceleration
  • ✓ Focus analytics generated and stored locally
  • ✓ Optional cloud sync uses zero-knowledge encryption
  • ✓ No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • ✓ Open security audit reports available
