How It Works

Three simple steps to better focus.

1

Download the App

Click the Download button for your operating system (macOS or Windows). Configure your settings and you're good to go!

2

Start a Session

Click Start to begin your session, align your camera view, and start working. BrainDock runs quietly in the background. You can pause or stop any time.

3

Report Generated

A PDF report containing session insights and analytics is available after each session ends.

Features

Timed Sessions

Start a timed session whenever you're ready. BrainDock helps you stay focused in real-time while you work, tracking your productive minutes and giving you a clear picture of each session.

Attention Coach

Gently notices when devices pull your attention away or when you step away from your desk. Receive helpful nudges to get back on track without breaking your flow.

Screen Awareness

Optionally identifies when you switch to distracting applications or websites. Helps you build better habits by understanding where your screen time actually goes.

Performance Report

Generate detailed PDF reports with insights into your session highlighting your focus scores, distractions & activity logs.
