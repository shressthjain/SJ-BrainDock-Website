Accessibility features

Built-in support so everyone can find their focus.

🎙️

Screen Reader Support

Full VoiceOver and NVDA support. Every feature, every setting, every notification - fully accessible.

⌨️

Keyboard Navigation

Complete keyboard access to all features. Navigate, configure, and control BrainDock entirely without a mouse.

🔤

Text Scaling

Respects system font size preferences. All text scales cleanly with your accessibility settings.

🎨

High Contrast

Optimized for both standard and high-contrast display modes. Clear visual hierarchy in every theme.

🔔

Customizable Notifications

Choose how you're notified - visual, audio, or haptic. Configure intensity, frequency, and style to suit your needs.

⏱️

Flexible Timing

No time-pressure interfaces. Timers and sessions adapt to your pace with generous defaults and easy customization.

Our commitment

Accessibility isn't a feature we bolt on - it's woven into everything we build. BrainDock is designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA compliance standards, ensuring that people of all abilities can use our tools effectively.

We conduct ongoing testing with assistive technologies, partner with disability advocacy groups, and actively seek feedback from our community to drive continuous improvement. Our goal is simple: focus should be available to everyone.

  • ✅ Tested with assistive tech
  • ✅ Community feedback driven
  • ✅ Regular accessibility audits
