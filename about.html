We're building the future of human attention

BrainDock was founded on a simple belief: technology should help us focus, not distract us. We're on a mission to give everyone the tools to do their best work.

Our mission

The average knowledge worker loses over 2 hours every day to distractions and context-switching. That's more than 500 hours a year - spent not on meaningful work, but on fighting for your own attention.

We started BrainDock because we believed there had to be a better way. Instead of asking people to simply "try harder" to focus, we built AI that actively protects your attention, coaches you toward better productivity habits, and helps you build lasting focus routines.

Everything we build is designed with privacy at the core. Your data stays on your device, your habits stay yours, and your focus belongs to you - not to advertisers or data brokers.

What we stand for

The principles that guide everything we build.

Privacy First

Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information. Focus is personal - your data should be too.

Focus Obsessed

Every feature, every pixel, every decision is made to protect your attention. If it doesn't help you focus, it doesn't ship.

Inclusive by Design

Accessibility isn't an afterthought - it's at the core of how we build. Focus tools should work for every mind and every ability.

Transparent

Honest pricing, no hidden fees, and an open roadmap. We believe trust is earned through transparency, not marketing.

Built by a team that cares

We're a small, distributed team of designers, engineers, and researchers united by a shared frustration with the attention economy - and a shared belief that we can fix it.
