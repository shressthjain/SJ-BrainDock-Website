Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

BrainDock may collect focus session data, application usage during sessions, and device information. If you create an account we collect email and credentials. Data is stored locally on your device by default.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to provide and improve BrainDock, generate focus reports, and personalize your experience. We do not sell your data. Anonymized, aggregated data may be used for product research.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync. We use encryption and industry-standard security best practices. You are responsible for securing your device and account credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for payments and authentication. They have their own privacy policies. We do not share your focus data with any third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Your Rights

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data under applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA). Manage your data in the app settings or contact privacy@braindock.app.

6. Children's Privacy

BrainDock is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact privacy@braindock.app or write to BrainDock, Inc., San Francisco, CA.
