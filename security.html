Privacy built in

BrainDock's Screen Awareness uses AI to detect distractions — here's exactly how your data flows, and why nothing is ever stored.

1

Screenshot captured locally

BrainDock periodically captures a screenshot of your active screen. This happens entirely on your device — the image stays in local memory.

2

Sent to Gemini for analysis

The screenshot is sent to Google's Gemini 3.0 Flash API over an encrypted connection. The AI analyzes it to determine whether you're focused or distracted.

3

Immediately discarded

Once the AI returns a focus/distraction result, the screenshot is permanently deleted. It is never stored on BrainDock's servers, and Google does not retain it under our data processing agreement.

4

Insights stay local

Your focus scores, session reports, and productivity analytics are generated and stored locally on your computer. Only you have access to your data.

Every privacy decision is in your hands. BrainDock gives you full control over what's monitored, stored, and shared.

Local-first architecture

All focus analytics, session data, and reports are generated and stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to BrainDock's servers.
